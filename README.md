# E-Cyclix – AI-Based E-Waste Management

E-Cyclix is an e-waste management solution designed to make electronic waste collection and disposal more accessible, particularly in rural and semi-urban communities.

## Problem

Electronic waste is often discarded without proper segregation or disposal because of limited awareness, inaccessible collection systems, and lack of incentives for users.

## Our Approach

E-Cyclix combines technology with a community-based collection model to make e-waste disposal easier and more rewarding.

The solution includes:

- Multilingual assistance for better accessibility
- Computer vision for identifying electronic waste and potential hazards
- Incentive-based participation to encourage responsible disposal
- Community and local collection support
- Digital reporting and tracking of e-waste
